**Final Objective:** Become the Supreme Ruler of Rongawi.

---

# GAMEPLAY RULES

### Relic-matching system

Players use five types of game pieces:

1. **Ambatron Emblem** – Charges Ambatron's power.
2. **Kendang** – Produces rhythmic energy and clears a line when combined.
3. **Kitab Pages** – Reveals clues and weakens enemy barriers.
4. **Iwak Tempe Essence** – Restores energy.
5. **Ambalabu Doll** – An obstacle that must be cleared through adjacent matches.

### Basic controls

- Drag one object to an adjacent position.
- Match three identical objects to activate their effect.
- Match four objects to produce an enhanced effect.
- Match five objects to trigger a powerful special ability.
- Complete the stage objective to progress through the story.

The board uses these same objects throughout the campaign, but each territory introduces different obstacles and rules.
